- **The lettering** is the tall, upright sans-serif road signs use: a rounded O, a curved S and a P with a round bowl, a third of the sign's height and centred inside the white border.
- **The face** is reflective sheeting rather than paint, so it stays a clean deep red and bright white, without the patch of worn-through paint that showed under the word.
- From the battle camera the sign still reads as a white word on a red octagon.
