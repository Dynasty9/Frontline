- **Squad names on the map** appear when they help: for the units you've selected or are pointing at, ones under fire, ones you've just given an order, and on the tactical map. The rest of the time the symbol and its strength say enough, and the battle isn't buried under names.
- **Pointing at a squad or vehicle** in the order of battle, or on the map, marks it with gold corner brackets, so you can find it at once.
- **The log** puts the same report from several units into one line: "Bravo 1, Charlie 1 and Delta 1 are moving out of the shellfire." That leaves room for the warnings that matter.

Scrolling the order of battle keeps the highlight on the row actually under your pointer, even when the pointer stays still. Reports from different units are grouped for four seconds after the first one. A repeat from the same unit is counted on its line, while a change of urgency and chat messages each get their own line.
