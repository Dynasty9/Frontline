- **The legs** are round columns splayed out to concrete piers, with two tiers of struts and crossed tension rods between them.
- **A catwalk** with a railing runs round the tank's foot.
- **The tank's plates** are in riveted bands, with rust weeping from the top seam, under a cone roof with a hatch, a vent and a finial.
- **A ladder** climbs a leg in its safety cage to the catwalk, and another goes up the tank to the roof.
