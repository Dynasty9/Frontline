- **Night is dark now.** In a night battle a Coalition soldier spots an enemy about 34 m off with his night sights,
  a Rebel only about 25 m. Vehicles see 80% of their usual range through thermal sights; aircraft and drones are
  unaffected.
- **What's lit is seen.** Anyone under a burning flare is seen as far as by day, on either side. Firing gives a man
  away for a moment too: his muzzle flash shows in the dark.
- **Star shells:** select a mortar team or a mortar pit and fire **Illum** (two shells, the second going up as the
  first burns down), or call the cheap **Illumination** fire support, ready from the start. Each flare bursts about
  70 m up, burns for 35 to 45 seconds, drifts on the wind and swings under its chute.
- **The AI fights by flarelight:** where its men hear fire they can't see, it puts a star shell up, aimed a little
  upwind so the flare drifts over the spot.
- The flare's light glows in the night haze, and the two nearest flares cast moving shadows.
