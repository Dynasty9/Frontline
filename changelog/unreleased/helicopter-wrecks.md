- **Broken rotors:** the blades shatter as they hit the ground. Some are left as stubs at the hub, others snap part
  way and bend down into the dirt, and some are flung clear. The tail rotor is usually gone.
- **How it lies:** a helicopter that spun in lies rolled onto its side with its nose dug in. One that autorotated to a
  heavy landing sits slumped on its belly where the undercarriage gave way.
- **Debris:** broken lengths of blade and torn panels lie scattered on the ground around the wreck.
- **Burnt out in its own paint:** the wreck keeps the helicopter's colours, blackening outwards from the engines as it
  burns, instead of turning into a flat black model.
