Small ferns grow around trees alongside fallen wood and brown-capped mushrooms. Roads, yards and water stay clear, and the detail follows the ground on slopes.

These are scenery, so they do not change cover or movement. The Grass setting controls their density and switches them off with the rest of the undergrowth. Existing birds and other wildlife retain their separate setting.
