- Switch between Battle, Squads, Map and Fires from the task bar. Each opens only the tools for that task.
- Search squads by callsign or role code, and vehicles by name. Click a result to select and locate it; double-click to zoom in.
- Battle keeps routine soldier names, speech and activity icons on selected troops. Squad markers, suppression warnings and hover inspection remain available.
- Selection details use the space freed when the minimap is closed.
