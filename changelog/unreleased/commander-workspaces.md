- The minimap stays visible in a compact command station, with three labelled icon buttons: Units, Map and Support.
- Units opens directly above the map. Search callsigns, role codes or vehicle names, then narrow to Infantry, Vehicles or units needing Attention. Click to locate and double-click to zoom.
- Support means artillery and air strikes. Commanders see supply costs and cooldowns; spectators get clearly labelled free demos.
- Each drawer includes short instructions for commanding or observing, and closes with its button or the close control.
- Normal battle views show selected squad plans. Open the tactical map for the wider army's plans and map layers. Routine counters stay compact; selected units retain their detailed status.
