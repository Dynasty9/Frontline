- The map stays fixed at the bottom left. Opening Units, Support or Layers never hides, moves or resizes it.
- A slim strip of labelled line icons replaces large tool tiles. Lists sit on soft edge shading, using the same typography and fine rules as the rest of Signal.
- Units provides search and Infantry, Vehicles or Attention filters. Click to locate, Shift-click to add and double-click to focus.
- Support means artillery and air strikes, with supply costs for commanders and clearly labelled free demos for spectators.
- Minimize the upper-right Events stream with its minus control. A small count tracks new arrivals, and the full message history remains available.
- Battlefield symbols and plan labels stay clear of occupied controls. Routine battle views show selected plans; Layers gives access to the wider tactical picture.
