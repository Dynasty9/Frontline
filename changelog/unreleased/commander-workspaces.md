- The minimap stays visible. Compact View, Squads, Expand, and Support icon buttons sit beneath it.
- Open one tool at a time. Search squads by callsign or role code and vehicles by name; click to locate and double-click to zoom.
- Support means off-map artillery and air strikes. Commanders see supply costs and cooldowns; spectators get clearly labelled free demos.
- Mode headings and short instructions explain commanding troops or observing the battle.
- Routine soldier names, speech, and activity icons belong to selected troops. Suppression warnings, squad markers, and hover inspection remain available.
