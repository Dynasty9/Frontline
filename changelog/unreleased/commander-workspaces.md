- The map stays fixed at the bottom left. Units and Support open in a dedicated workspace on the right, keeping navigation separate from task work.
- Search is ready to type as soon as Units opens. Find callsigns, role names or vehicles, then use arrow keys to browse. Infantry, Vehicles and Attention filters narrow the list.
- Click selects without moving your camera. Double-click, Enter or Focus selected brings the unit into view and returns you to the battle, with orders ready. Escape closes the workspace before clearing selection.
- Taller roster rows separate names and strength from status. Support shows costs and cooldowns, explains insufficient supply, and closes when you choose a strike so you can place its target.
- Events yields while you use a task workspace and restores your previous preference afterward. It can also be minimized independently without losing history.
- The battle interface now follows the menus: flat translucent surfaces, open edges and fine rules. Status and observer controls sit directly on soft edge shading; action shortcuts stay quiet until needed.
- Selected soldiers and vehicles appear in portraits drawn from their in-game models. A three-column command grid pairs action icons with names and keyboard shortcuts; active targeting orders are highlighted.
- Observer controls share the same design. Empty selection shows a compact hint that lets battlefield clicks pass through. Single-unit details fit their content instead of stretching across the screen. At large interface scales, portraits yield and task headings compact so unit rows remain usable. Battlefield labels and director captions stay clear of the deck.
- The command deck now uses shared components for consistent panels, icons, shortcuts, action states and portraits. Build and training actions use a building icon; active targeting actions highlight even when no shortcut is assigned.
