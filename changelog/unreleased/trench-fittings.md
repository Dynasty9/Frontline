- **Parapets:** the front of every fire bay is faced with sandbags, two bags high, with loopholes in the top course and steel loophole plates behind some of them. Some bays have a low parados of bags at the back.
- **Revetment:** walls are held up with planks behind posts, corrugated iron on pickets or woven wattle hurdles, varying from bay to bay.
- **Along the bays:** scaling ladders, ammunition boxes on the fire step, a sentry's periscope over the parapet, water cans and the field telephone line pegged along the back wall.
- **Dugouts:** some rear bays lead down to a dugout, a timber doorway under a roof of logs and earth with a gas blanket over the door. Shelled squads go below, leaving a sentry on the parapet, and are back on the fire step when the barrage lifts, the enemy comes within 90 m, or you give them an order. A shell that brings the section down buries the entrance, and the men dig themselves out hurt. Right-click a dugout's doorway to send a squad below yourself; it stays there until you order it out or the enemy comes on. At night a little lamplight shows at the foot of the stairs.
- **Machine-gun bays:** a gun placed on a trench is now roofed over with logs, earth and bags, closed at the sides and open to the front.
- **Manning the line:** a squad told to defend a trench line spreads along the fire steps of the bays facing the enemy, and moving along a trench no longer cuts corners over the parapet.
