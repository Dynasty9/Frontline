- **Shoring:** walls are raw earth where the clay stands and shored where it doesn't, with whatever was to hand: cut logs stacked behind stakes, pallets stood on edge, odd planks, a sheet of OSB or roofing iron.
- **Bags:** loose heaps at two or three firing points along each fire bay, olive, tan and white polypropylene all caked in mud, with a few more lying about on the spoil.
- **Clutter:** the floor collects ammunition tins, water bottles, ration packs, cardboard, a jerrycan and the odd spent launcher tube. Duckboards are laid in some stretches and not others, and the cut faces and spoil are lumpier.
- **Dugouts:** some rear bays lead down to a dugout through a frame of round logs with a tarpaulin hung over the door. Shelled squads go below, leaving a sentry on the parapet, and are back on the fire step when the barrage lifts, the enemy comes within 90 m, or you give them an order. A shell that brings the section down buries the entrance, and the men dig themselves out hurt. Right-click a dugout's doorway to send a squad below yourself; it stays there until you order it out or the enemy comes on.
- **Manning the line:** a squad told to defend a trench line spreads along the fire steps of the bays facing the enemy, and moving along a trench no longer cuts corners over the parapet.
