- **Orders step aside.** A new order shows its route for a moment as you give it, the squad answers, and then it's
  out of sight. Select a unit or rest the cursor on it (on the ground, its map symbol or its line in the order of
  battle) to see its orders again; the tactical map (Tab) shows everyone's.
- **Hover a squad** for its card: callsign and type, how many are fit to fight, their nerve, their ammunition, what
  they were told and what they're doing, and the man under the cursor. Vehicles show their role, damage and who's
  aboard.
- **The command net.** Radio traffic - your XO, the mission, other commanders - is heard one transmission at a time
  as a subtitle low in the middle of the screen, with a burst of squelch, instead of piling up in the corner.
  Squads answer your orders on it, and vehicles in their own words: a crew moves, a helicopter is inbound, a boat
  gets under way.
- **Fewer notifications.** Three lines at most; the same news again counts up on one line instead of stacking, and
  routine news (a squad trained, a building finished) passes quickly.
- **The order of battle** is grouped into infantry, support, and land, air and sea vehicles, and stays quiet until
  a unit needs you: routing, pinned, low on ammunition, in contact. Click a heading to select every unit under it.
  It, and the fire missions below it, step back while you watch the battle and come up as your cursor goes to them
  (Settings > Interface > Quiet side panels).
- **Finished objectives** fold into one line after a few seconds, so the list holds what's still to do.
- **The doctrine tree** (Y, or the Research Post) replaces the two big research tiers: steps in four branches -
  land, air, sea and the infantry's kit - from mechanised infantry to armour, air defence, artillery and robots,
  from army aviation to attack helicopters and drones, from riverine boats to a patrol ship. Every vehicle in the
  game is on it, and each locked vehicle says which step it needs. Click a lit step to research it.
- **The AI commander climbs the tree too:** it builds its Research Post earlier and saves for the next step, so
  its carriers, tanks, air defence, rocket lorries and boats now turn up in a long battle.
