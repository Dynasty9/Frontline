Every subject offers a rear and aerial view. Vehicles with tracks or wheels have a low running-gear view. Models can also carry cameras on the barrel, beside the roof gun and over the engine deck. Rotorcraft add a cabin-side view and boats add a bow view. The tanks use the same mounts used in the one-minute ridge film. Change the key in Settings under Controls.
