- **The walls** are tall HESCO cells, four to a side. One cell is left out at the back as the way in.
- **A baffle of bags** stands outside the way in, so nothing fired at it goes straight through.
- **The roof** is corrugated sheets on timber bearers, under a burst layer of sandbags.
- The net, the mast, the sandbag walls and the stores are as before, and the FOB gives the same cover.
