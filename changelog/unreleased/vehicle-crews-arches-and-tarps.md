- Every helicopter shows its crew through clear cockpit glass, in helicopter seats with an instrument panel
  across the cockpit, and the Xi'an's stepped cockpits show their two crew. In flight a faint dark rotor disc
  shows the blades turning, in both renderers.
- Aircraft carry their side's colour as a band across each wingtip, clear of the national roundel or star.
- Wheeled carriers (the BTR, Marshall, Centauro, Gorgon and Marid) turn their wheels in proper wheel arches
  instead of hanging them under a flat side.
- Tank turrets, carrier roofs and truck cabs carry their crews' kit: bundles, rolled tarps, ammunition cans and
  tow cables, different from vehicle to vehicle. Engine decks have fine louvres, perforated covers or mesh by
  army, and slat cages are dense flat bar.
- Tarps over loads take the shape of the boxes under them and are tied down to the bed, crates carry lettering
  instead of a painted bar, and truck tilts show their bows and panels from the battle camera.
- Car and carrier body panels are smooth pressed sheets with no seam lines, their arch trims are moulded, and
  tyres are black rubber. Clear cab glass is faintly tinted, sun shades fold properly, and buggy seats have
  harness belts.
- The SUV stands tall with dark cladding and wraparound lights, the MB 4WD wears a hardtop or a top that suits
  its paint, the quad bike and kart are moulded, and the ambulance has blinds behind its rear windows.
- Cockpits, cab interiors and many fittings cost fewer vertices: the roster is lighter than before these
  changes.
