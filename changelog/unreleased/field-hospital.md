- **A frame tent** with canvas walls under a pitched roof, its frame's arches showing through as seams. Clear windows run down both sides with their blinds rolled up.
- **The door** at one end is open, with the flap rolled up over it and boards laid for a step.
- **Red crosses** on white mark both slopes of the roof and the door, and a flag with the cross flies beside the tent.
- **Guy ropes** run out to pegs. By the door are stretchers on trestles and crates of medical stores, with oxygen cylinders at the far end.
- The Coalition's tent is new olive drab. The Rebels' is old khaki, faded and patched. It works as before.
