- **The walls** have a brick dado to knee height under a concrete coping. The sheeting above it has corner flashings.
- **Glazing** runs in a band high along the sides and back to light the shop floor.
- **Rainwater** runs off the roof into gutters along the side eaves and down a downpipe at each corner.
- **At the front**, hazard-yellow bollards guard the roller shutter's jambs. Beside it is a personnel door with a sheeted canopy.
- **Gas bottles** for the welders are chained to the back wall, and drums stand down one side.
