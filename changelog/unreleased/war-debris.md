- **Debris** spills from the walls of a house that's a ruin, or that has just been shelled, set on fire or lost a wall: lumps of brick and render, flakes of plaster, shards of roof tile, a splintered beam, glass. It's thickest by the wall and thins out over a metre and a half.
- **Broken glass** lies on the ground under most windows whose panes have been shot out.
- It all lies flat: men walk over it and it gives no cover.
