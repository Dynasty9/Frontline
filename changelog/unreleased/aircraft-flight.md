- **Gun runs** (A-10C, Wipeout, Buzzard, Neophron) aim the nose cannon with the whole aircraft. They push over
  into a shallow dive on the target, fire, walk the burst through it by easing the nose up, then pull off in a
  climbing turn. Before, they fired while flying almost level just above the ground.
- **Bomb runs** hold steady and level for the drop, then break away in a hard, steeply banked climb instead of
  flying straight on and vanishing.
- **Gunships and drones** fly in wings level, roll into their orbit, and roll out to fly off climbing. The orbit's
  bank is now the 45 degrees its circle needs, down from an overbanked 59.
- Over hills, every aircraft climbs smoothly ahead of high ground instead of jumping up when it reaches it.
- They swing in onto their run along a curved approach, banking through the bend, rather than appearing on
  the line.
- Underneath it is a stick-and-throttle flight model: planes roll, pull g, gain speed in a dive, bleed it in a
  hard turn and can't hold their height below stall speed. The support pilots fly it by autopilot today; it is
  built so a player can take the stick later.
- The nose rides a little above the flight path, more so in hard turns, as a wing needs to lift.
