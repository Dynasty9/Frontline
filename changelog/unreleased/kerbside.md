- **Town street lamps** are steel columns with a service door at the base and an arm sweeping out to a cast lantern, with a socket joining the two. **Village lamps** hang an enamelled shade from a bracket on a wooden pole.
- **The square's lanterns** are fluted cast posts, each with a ladder bar and a glazed lantern under its roof and finial.
- **Hydrants** have their bolted flange, bonnet, and outlet caps on chains.
- **Stop signs** keep their lettering in front of the face at oblique angles and read STOP in a white border. They stand off their posts on brackets, with bare galvanised backs.
- **Mailboxes** sit on their posts with the flag up or down, and **the electricity cabinet** has louvres, doors and a danger plate.
