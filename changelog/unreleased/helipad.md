- **The Coalition's pad** is aluminium landing mat laid in a bond, with ramp panels round its edge. **The Rebels'** is concrete, cracked and patched.
- **A painted touchdown circle** goes round the H, with halved tyres painted white marking the edge and tie-down rings.
- **Round its corners** are a windsock lifting on its mast, a fuel bladder on its berm with its hose and pump, chocks and a fire extinguisher. The windsocks follow the same weather wind even when the pads face different directions.
