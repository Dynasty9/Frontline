The captured rifle controller keeps partial support until its existing foot-plant release begins, reducing the acceleration back toward the moving animation. The reach-release distance, arm and leg lengths, ground clearance and correction speed limits remain unchanged. This reduces measured planted-foot sliding, particularly backward and sideways, but does not eliminate it.
