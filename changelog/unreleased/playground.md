- **Swings** hang on galvanised chains from their A-frames, one seat often a toddler's cradle.
- **The slide** has a tower with a deck, guard rails and a little roof, a ladder of round rungs, and a steel chute between raised sides.
- **A climbing dome** of curved tube replaces the square frame.
- **The seesaw** sits on a sprung pivot, with seats, grab handles and a tyre half sunk under each end.
- **The sandpit** has timber kerbs with a seat board round them, the sand heaped and dug, and a bucket and spade left in it.
- **The roundabout** has its painted disc and grab rails.
