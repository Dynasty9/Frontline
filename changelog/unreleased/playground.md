- **Swings** hang from a heavy top beam on A-frames of thick tube, on galvanised chain, one seat often a
  toddler's cradle seat.
- **The slide** is a tower with a deck, moulded side panels with portholes and a pitched roof, a ladder with
  hand loops at the top and a bar to make you sit, and a short, steep stainless chute in coloured sides.
- **The climbing dome** is a geodesic frame with some of its triangles filled with panels.
- **The roundabout** is a low disc in two colours with grab rails bent down from its hub and a handrail
  ring, where it was a parasol of struts.
- **The seesaw** rests with one end down on the tyre half sunk under each seat.
- **The sandpit** has seats across its corners, raked sand with a hole dug and a heap beside it, castles
  turned out of the bucket and a spade stuck in the sand.
- **Spring riders**, a horse or a duck, stand behind the seesaw and by the slide.
- On a smaller plot the equipment closes up rather than standing in itself: the roundabout no longer runs
  into the climbing frame or the sandpit.
