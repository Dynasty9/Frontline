- **Pick it** under Settings > Lighting & Look > Time of day & weather, in the menu's Time and weather row, or in photo mode. A random battle snows now and then, about one in twenty.
- **The light** is flat and cold: a weak sun with barely any shadow, a pale blue-grey sky, and haze closing in.
- **Snowflakes** drift down with the wind and flutter as they fall, staying put in the world as you pan through them.
- **The ground** lies white on the flat and stays dark on steep slopes. Shell holes and the earth they throw up show as dark mud and grey slush, and tracks leave dark lines through it. Dirt roads become slush lanes between white verges; town streets keep black wheel paths and heaped white gutters.
- **Everything standing in it** gathers snow on its upper faces: roofs, window ledges, tank decks, sandbags, camouflage nets, trench spoil, trees, bushes and grass. Vehicles shake some off, so their colours still show.
- Snow that starts mid-battle settles over a few minutes and thaws slowly when it stops. The deep snow muffles the wind and the distant battle.
- It's looks only: nothing sees, moves or burns any differently in snow. It works in the browser too.
