- **Front gardens** with room for it have a bed of flowers in two or three colours, or a few shrubs (some flowering), kept clear of the door, the bins and the pots.
- **Clipped hedges** run along the front of nearly half the unfenced gardens, with gaps for the path, the drive and the mailbox.
- **Shrubs** grow at the back corners of many houses.
- They're built from the trees' photographed leaves, and are scenery: a shell or a passing truck flattens them, and they give no cover.
