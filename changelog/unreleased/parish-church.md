- **The church** stands back from one of the roads into the town, its tower to the road. It has a nave of four bays between stepped buttresses with tall pointed windows, a lower chancel with three lancets in its east end, and a west tower. The tower has a pointed door, a clock on two faces, louvred belfry openings, battlements, pinnacles and an octagonal copper spire with a cross on top.
- **The churchyard** round it has a low stone wall (cover, like a garden wall) with a gate gap for the path to the door, rows of graves either side and behind, and a dark conifer at each front corner.
- It's solid stone. Men take cover against its walls, nobody goes in, it blocks sight and movement, and enough fire brings it down to a heap of rubble.
- Missions' towns are unchanged; a skirmish map with no room by its roads has no church.
