- **The church** stands back from one of the roads into the town, its tower to the road: a limewashed nave of five bays with tall pointed windows between stone buttresses, a lower chancel, and a solid west tower with stone quoins, a clock on two faces, louvred belfry openings, battlements, pinnacles and a slate spire with a cross.
- **You can go in**: its south door opens like a house's, the windows are chest high to fire from, and the roofs lift off when your men are inside, over the pews, the font and the altar. Its walls give cover and come down to rubble like a house's.
- **The churchyard** round it has a low stone wall (cover, like a garden wall) with a gate gap, rows of graves either side and behind, and a dark conifer at each front corner.
- Missions' towns are unchanged; a skirmish map with no room by its roads has no church.
