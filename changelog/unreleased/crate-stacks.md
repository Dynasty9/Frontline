- **Shipping crates** have their boards in a frame of battens, a diagonal brace across each long face, cleats over the lid and two steel bands round them. Their ends are stencilled with a lot number and the this-way-up arrows. Most are bare pine and about a third are painted olive drab.
- **Ammunition boxes** are olive boards with a lighter lid, hasps at the front, rope handles through cleats at the ends, and a yellow high-explosive band and lot number on the front.
- **Each load stands on a pallet**: a low crate with two boxes on it, three layers of boxes with the top layer laid across, or a tall crate. On a long stack, a box is sometimes left lying on top of the pile.
- The stacks give the same cover as before.
