- **Ground mist** gathers in hollows, along rivers and round ponds, thickest at dawn and rising off wet ground
  after rain, torn into wisps by the wind. A **haze** lies higher up; the sun lights it, so clouds throw shafts of
  shadow through it.
- **The weather varies across the map**: rain comes over in heavier and lighter bands, shells and vehicles raise
  dust from dry ground, and barrages and fires leave a pall of smoke that drifts away downwind and thins out.
- It all eases off as you zoom out, so it never hides the fighting you're watching. It's scenery only: it doesn't
  change who can see whom.
- **Caustics**: the light the ripples focus dances across sandy and muddy beds in the shallows (from Medium water
  quality, finer from High), and from High it's thrown back up onto hulls, piers and banks just above the water.
- The browser version gets a lighter version of the mist and smoke through its distance fog, and the simpler caustics.
