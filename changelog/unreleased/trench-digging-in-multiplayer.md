+++
title = "Trenches dig in properly for joined players"
summary = "In multiplayer, a trench being dug or shelled no longer stalls the battlefield for players who joined someone else's battle. Its depth, cover and fighting positions now keep up with the host's."
kind = "fixed"
+++

- While a trench was being dug or shelled, players who had joined a battle could see units and vehicles
  hitch: each update that changed the trench's depth stopped part-way through.
- The trench itself now deepens, fills in under shellfire and offers its cover for them as it does for the host.
