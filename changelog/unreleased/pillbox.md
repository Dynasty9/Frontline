- **The walls** are one pour of weathered concrete with the marks of the plank formwork on them, their corners cut back. The old flat blue-grey boxes are gone.
- **The roof** is a slab half a metre thick with a chamfered edge, its top falling away to every side, moss grown in patches where the rain stands. On it are a steel observation cupola with vision slits, the escape hatch and a vent. The loose rows of sandbags are gone.
- **Every firing slit** sits in a concrete frame that splays out from it, with steel angle on its lips. The slits and the doorway look into a dark room, not lit concrete.
- **At the back** the steel doors stand open, folded against the wall, with step irons up to the roof and weeds at the foot.
- **A season in the line:** grime under the slab's edge and bullet strikes round the slits.
- The bunker gives the same protection as before. The Rebels' log dugout is unchanged.
