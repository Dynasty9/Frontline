- **The roof** is a thick slab stepped out over the walls. On it are a layer of turf, with the concrete lip showing round it, and a parapet of bags two high along the front.
- **Every firing slit** has a concrete hood over it, a sill sloped to drain, cheeks splayed at its ends and steel angle round the opening.
- **On the roof** are the escape hatch on its kerb, a periscope looking over the parapet and a mushroom vent cowl.
- **Rain** has stained the walls: dark streaks run down under the spouts at the back and fainter ones under the roof's edge.
- The bunker gives the same protection as before. The Rebels' log dugout is unchanged.
