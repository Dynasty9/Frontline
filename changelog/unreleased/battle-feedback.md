- **Moments:** an enemy vehicle destroyed, a squad wiped out, a town or a FOB taken or lost shows for a few
  seconds under the status line: green for good news, red for bad, with the grid. Space (or a click on it) takes
  you there.
- **Kills and losses** on the status line: the men and vehicles your side has killed and lost so far.
- **Idle squads:** squads of yours standing about with nothing to do are counted above the fire missions. Press
  `.` (or click it) to select the next one and go there.
- **Under fire:** a squad of yours that comes under fire where you aren't looking is reported once in the log,
  with a ping on the map, so Space takes you to it.
- **Fire missions:** the list shows at most three ready missions and folds the rest into one line
  (`+9 MORE · 6 RDY`) that opens them all, instead of filling the left of the screen.
- **Squad cards:** what each man is doing fits his cell as a short code (`SMOKING`, `FALL BK`); hover it for the
  full words.
- No two men of a squad share a name any more.
