- Each side's markings (roof and hull chevrons, wingtip bands) are a faded, matt tactical paint near the hull's
  own shade: still blue or red, no longer the brightest thing on the vehicle. The Little Bird's and Buzzard's are
  painted on the skin instead of standing off it.
- Truck cabs no longer carry an orange or pink recognition panel, and the panels other vehicles carry are folded
  and faded.
- Canopies show the sky on their glass while still showing the crew, armoured windscreens and vision blocks read
  as dark glass instead of black holes, and helicopter and drone rotor discs show again from above.
- The pickup's tonneau is dark matt vinyl, the MB 4WD's soft top is dark vinyl again, the SUV's windscreen
  shows its dash, the tractor's cab doors are glazed, and the MB 4WD's bonnet and the van's roof lose their
  hot white spots.
- Truck beds under their tilts are in shade, sun-faded tilt widths stay khaki, the HEMTT's ammunition load is
  stacked boxes again, and the technical's door plate is steel, not a hole.
- Fixes: the T-55's drum brackets no longer hang in the air without their drum, cable coils lie flat and dark,
  the Zamak's grille has its bold slats back, the Scorcher's roof kit sits in rails, the Panther's bags are
  strapped, and the Tempest's crane no longer sinks into its load.
