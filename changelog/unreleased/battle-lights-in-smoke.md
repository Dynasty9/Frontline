- **Smoke lit from inside:** a shell bursting in or near smoke lights the cloud for an instant, brightest on the side
  facing the flash, then lets it fall dark again. Before, the smoke stayed dark beside the glow of the flash.
- **Fires and lamps:** a burning wreck or house, a flare or a street lamp lights the smoke drifting past it, and a
  gun's flash lights its own smoke.
- **Tracers:** each tracer leaves a faint streak of its colour in the air it flies through, brighter through dust,
  smoke and mist.
- The browser version lights its smoke the same way. The tracers' streaks in the air need volumetric fog.
