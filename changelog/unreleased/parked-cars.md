- **Parking bays** are painted on the road at the kerb in a few short runs along the main streets by the shops, and about half of them have a car in. Every other car in town stands in a driveway or a car park, not in the street.
- **Left where they were parked**: most face the way the traffic ran on their side of the street, and now and then one has burnt out in its bay.
- **Where nobody parks**: no bay is painted in a junction's mouth, on a zebra, across a driveway, by a hydrant or at the bus stop, nor opposite another. A lorry or a tank still gets down the street, though it would rather go round than shove through.
- **For the fighting**: riflemen take cover behind the cars, and they catch fire and burn like the cars in the drives.
- Battles only: the missions' towns are as they were.
