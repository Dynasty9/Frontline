- **Runs of parked cars** stand nose to tail along the kerbs, with gaps between. The main streets near the shops are the fullest.
- **Left in a hurry**: most face the way the traffic ran on their side of the street. The odd one is slewed out into the lane, and about one in seven has burnt out where it stood.
- **Where nobody parks**: never in a junction's mouth, on a zebra, across a driveway or at the bus stop, and never opposite another car. A lorry or a tank still gets down the street, though it would rather go round than shove through.
- **For the fighting**: riflemen take cover behind them, and they catch fire and burn like the cars in the drives.
- Battles only: the missions' towns are as they were.
