Locally installed Mixamo prone clips replace procedural crawling and the frozen prone idle. Crawl playback follows movement speed, stops settle into idle, and a captured recovery returns the rifleman to crouched movement. Idle and firing keep the support hand on the rifle while hit reactions recover.

The optional assets remain local; installations without them retain the fallback. Prone magazine timing still needs clip-specific calibration. These fixed-step recordings show a controlled animation fixture, not battlefield performance or uneven-terrain validation.
