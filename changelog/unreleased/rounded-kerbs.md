- **Corners** curve round on kerbstones laid in an arc, the sidewalk following them and the asphalt filling the bend.
- The corners still follow the lie of the land, and grass still keeps off them.
- The line along the middle of the road no longer shows a **thin dark stripe** at zebra crossings, which was plainest under street lights at night.
