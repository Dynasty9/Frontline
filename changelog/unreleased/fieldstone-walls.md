- **Field stones** of every size, laid longer than tall, make up the stone walls round gardens and along the fields.
- **Each stone is rounded** and its own shade of grey or brown, with now and then a warmer one, and is pointed in darker mortar that wanders and thickens at the corners.
- **Along the top**, field stones of the same kind are set on edge across the wall, a tall one and a short one in turn, leaning a little and bedded in mortar, so the wall ends in a ragged line of stones rather than a smooth stone lid.
- They give the same cover as before.
- Fieldstone uses its own material pattern so it can coexist with the playground's poured rubber surface.
