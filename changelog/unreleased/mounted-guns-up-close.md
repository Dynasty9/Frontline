- **Each army's own guns:**
  - NATO's remote stations carry the .50 and the Mk 19.
  - CSAT's carry the Kord and the AGS-17 with its finned barrel.
  - The DShK has its anti-aircraft ring sight.
  - The 7.62s are slim like the real M240, with a folded bipod. The PK has its skeleton stock.
  - The .50 is the real width, with proper spade grips.
- **Remote weapon stations** elevate as one: the gun, its sight head (thermal, camera and laser windows under an armoured door) and its ammunition box move together, and a chute of links arches into the gun's feed.
- **Armoured glass** looks a hand thick: dark green blocks set deep behind the steel, in the Humvee's turret, the Abrams loader's gun shield and the armed speedboat's after gun.
- **Muzzles** match the real guns: flash hiders on the ZU-23, KPVT and 40 mm Bofors, a plain muzzle on the Bradley-pattern 25 mm, and the Gepard-pattern velocity sensor on the twin 35 mm.
- **Gun mounts**:
  - Ammunition feeds from the side each gun really feeds from.
  - Tank roof guns sit at a loader's chest height, with their ammunition.
  - Cannon housings and coaxial guns rise with the gun instead of letting it poke through.
  - Boat guns stand on braced pedestals behind shields.
  - Aircraft fire through port plugs and rubber boots.
  - The Ghost Hawk's door guns swing out on arms, fed from inside the cabin.
- **The Panther's grenades** now leave its grenade launcher, not the machine gun beside it.
