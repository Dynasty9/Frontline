- **The war memorial** stands on three granite steps. Its pedestal carries a bronze plaque of names on each face, and on top a bronze soldier, larger than life, rests on his rifle with his head bowed. Wreaths of laurel and poppies lean against its front.
- **The park's memorial** is an obelisk with its capstone and a bronze wreath, on the same steps.
- **Market stalls** have a timber frame under a pitched canopy of striped canvas, scalloped at the eaves, over a trestle of crates heaped with produce and a set of scales.
