- **The war memorial** stands on three granite steps. Its pedestal carries a bronze plaque of names on each face, and on top a bronze soldier, larger than life, rests on his arms reversed: his rifle upside down before him, his hands folded on its butt, his head bowed under his helmet, in his greatcoat and pack. The bronze has gone green with weather.
- **Poppy wreaths** stand on the top step, leaning against the foot of the pedestal.
- **The park's memorial** is an obelisk with its capstone and a bronze laurel wreath, on the same steps.
- **Market stalls** have a timber frame under a pitched canopy of striped canvas, scalloped at the eaves, over a trestle of crates heaped with produce and a set of scales.
