The skinned rifleman no longer pulls his hands back onto a slung rifle during smoking, chatting, and seated idle poses. The local animation viewer can preview the downloaded work and vehicle clips without attaching a rifle to their hands. These new clips are preview assets; vehicle boarding and construction gameplay still use their existing presentation.
