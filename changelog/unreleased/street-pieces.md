- **The square's fountain** is a basin of dressed stone with a moulded lip, with water in it and in two bowls on a pedestal and stem, weed at their foot.
- **Bus shelters** have a steel frame, glazed back and ends (now and then a pane shot out), a curved roof, a slatted bench, a timetable case and the stop's flag.
- **Planters** on the square are concrete troughs of bedding flowers round a clipped box ball.
- **Litter bins** are slatted drums on posts.
- **Each road into town** has a yellow name board with the town's name and its district under it.
