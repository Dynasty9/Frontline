- The Humvee's armoured windows show its cab: two front seats either side of the wide tunnel with the radios on
  it, the dash and steering wheel, the rear seats and the gunner's step. Wrecks keep their old solid cab.
- The Merkava, and the vehicles built on its hull, carry the top of the track on four return rollers a side, and
  the track hangs a little between them. They sit behind the skirts, as on the real tank.
- Canvas tilts on the HEMTT, M35, Zamak, Tempest and other trucks hang between their bows: each bow shows as a
  ridge and the eave line scallops between them.
- The 4x4's tailgate spare costs fewer vertices: the face against the tailgate, which nobody sees, is built plainly.
