The first four recordings were published by qubodup. All sources were taken from the publisher's high-quality previews, mixed to mono and resampled to 32 kHz. Engine and rolling loops have embedded loop points. The diesel recording represents a heavy engine for the game.

The Abrams rolling loop uses [Moving tank](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/602145/moving-tank), a public-domain U.S. Army recording by Lt. Col. John Hall. The 1.12–8.82 s pass was mixed to mono, filtered to reduce microphone wind and crossfaded. The other tracked vehicles retain the sand recording. The turbine loop has a narrow reduction near 2.84 kHz so the rolling links remain audible.
