`eroded_face_*` (layer 9) is original: `tools/bake_alpine_assets.py eroded_face` runs seeded
rainfall erosion on a tiling rock slope and packs its gullies, shade and normals. No source assets are used.

The original 2K JPEG diffuse and OpenGL normal maps are retained as baking inputs.
Additional CC0 displacement, ambient-occlusion and roughness maps are downloaded
only by the offline baker. Source URLs and checksums are recorded in sources.json.

`tools/bake_alpine_assets.py` produces 1K mipmapped material pairs:
- `*_color_height.png`: sRGB diffuse RGB, linear displacement in alpha.
- `*_surface.png`: linear OpenGL normal XY, ambient occlusion B, roughness A.

The rock-face pair also includes original procedural slab joints baked into its
height, normals, occlusion, colour and roughness. No additional source assets are used.

Alpha is material data, not transparency. Import keeps all four channels, disables
alpha-border repair and generates mipmaps. The runtime keeps the material pairs
in two texture arrays with independent mip chains, so adding snow and forest litter
does not exhaust the browser renderer's texture-sampler budget.

`alpine_forms.tres` contains three original analytic terrain forms (buttress,
cirque and saddle), generated by that script. They are heightfield stamps, not
scanned cliff meshes or overhangs. Standard maps place them deterministically
before grading and erosion, so rendering and authoritative terrain agree.

All runtime assets are bundled; the game makes no texture downloads.

## Surveyed Alpine ridge shapes

`surveyed_alps.res` contains modified elevation stamps around Mont Blanc,
Matterhorn and Jungfrau. SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey;
Terrain Tiles processed by Mapzen and hosted by the AWS Open Data program.
The zoom-12 tiles use the public-domain SRTM dataset in these regions.

- [Terrain Tiles dataset](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/)
- [Source datasets and zoom levels](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/data-sources.md)
- [Source attribution and terms](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md)

`surveyed_sources.json` pins the 27 source tile URLs and SHA-256 checksums.
`tools/bake_surveyed_terrain.py` decodes the Terrarium elevations, crops each
512-pixel mountain group, subtracts its lower foothills, normalizes its relief
and smoothly lowers its border to zero. The game rotates and scales these
stamps into fictional surrounding ranges; they are not geographic replicas.
The bundled resource preserves 32-bit float heights. Source tiles and temporary
baking files are not needed at runtime. USGS and Mapzen do not endorse these
modifications or this game.

## Trench soil

[Brown Mud](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud), by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven, CC0.
The scan covers 1.3 metres and supplies trench clods and fine soil grains (array layer 10).
The offline baker packs its colour, height, OpenGL normal, occlusion and roughness maps;
original download URLs and verified checksums are in sources.json.
