The original 2K JPEG diffuse and OpenGL normal maps are retained as baking inputs.
Additional CC0 displacement, ambient-occlusion and roughness maps are downloaded
only by the offline baker. Source URLs and checksums are recorded in sources.json.

`tools/bake_alpine_assets.py` produces 1K mipmapped material pairs:
- `*_color_height.png`: sRGB diffuse RGB, linear displacement in alpha.
- `*_surface.png`: linear OpenGL normal XY, ambient occlusion B, roughness A.

The rock-face pair also includes original procedural slab joints baked into its
height, normals, occlusion, colour and roughness. No additional source assets are used.

Alpha is material data, not transparency. Import keeps all four channels, disables
alpha-border repair and generates mipmaps. The runtime uses the same number of
texture reads as the earlier colour/normal pair, with additional surface relief.

`alpine_forms.tres` contains three original analytic terrain forms (buttress,
cirque and saddle), generated by that script. They are heightfield stamps, not
scanned cliff meshes or overhangs. Standard maps place them deterministically
before grading and erosion, so rendering and authoritative terrain agree.

All runtime assets are bundled; the game makes no texture downloads.
